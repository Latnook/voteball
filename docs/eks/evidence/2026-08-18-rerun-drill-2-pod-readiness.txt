Zero non-200 responses. The PDB (min=1, allowed=1) is what makes this safe: it permits exactly
one voluntary disruption, so the second replica must stay serving. With a single replica the
PDB would make draining IMPOSSIBLE rather than safe -- you cannot evict the last pod without
violating it, so it blocks until the node is force-killed. That is the reason for min 2 nodes
and 2 replicas, not redundancy for its own sake.
