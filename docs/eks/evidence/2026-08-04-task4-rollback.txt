## The recursion bound holding (build #6, depth 1)

## Why this matters
Without the ROLLBACK_DEPTH bound, previous-tag.sh returns the tag before the current one --
so a failed rollback makes the failed tag 'previous' again and the two oscillate forever,
each cycle pushing a commit to master and rolling production pods. Builds #5 and #6 show the
chain terminating after exactly one rollback attempt, as designed.
